Customers decide quickly whether to contact a business, and photos play a huge role in that decision. Clear, honest images of your work and premises show professionalism and help customers picture what you'll do for them. The good news: modern smartphones are more than capable — you just need a few techniques.

## Clean your lens

It sounds obvious, but a smudged lens is the most common reason phone photos look hazy. Wipe it with a soft cloth before every shoot.

## Use natural light

Light makes the biggest difference to photo quality.

- **Shoot during the day** and use daylight wherever possible.
- **Avoid harsh midday sun** outdoors; early morning and late afternoon light is softer.
- **Indoors, face the window.** Place your subject so window light falls on it, and turn off mixed yellow lighting if you can.
- **Avoid the flash** in most situations — it creates harsh shadows and reflections.

## Keep it steady and level

- Hold your phone with both hands and brace your elbows against your body.
- Turn on the camera's grid lines to keep horizons and walls straight.
- For interiors, hold the phone at chest height and keep it vertical to avoid leaning walls.

## Clear the clutter

Before you take a photo, remove anything distracting — tools, packaging, bins, cables and personal items. A tidy space looks more professional and lets customers focus on what matters.

## Compose with intention

- **Fill the frame** with your subject rather than leaving lots of empty space.
- **Use the rule of thirds:** place the main subject along the grid lines rather than dead centre.
- **Shoot from different angles:** straight on, at 45 degrees, and close-up details.
- **Show scale:** include a hand or a familiar object for small products.

## Tell a story with before-and-after photos

For trades and services — plumbing, painting, landscaping, cleaning, beauty, panel beating — before-and-after photos are extremely persuasive. Take both from the **same position and angle** so the comparison is clear.

## Photograph your team and premises

Customers like to know who they'll be dealing with. A friendly photo of your team, your shopfront, signage or branded vehicle builds trust. Always ask team members for permission before sharing their photos.

## Respect privacy

- Ask clients before photographing their homes or faces.
- Avoid capturing house numbers, vehicle registration plates or personal documents.
- Never share photos of patients, children or sensitive situations without explicit, appropriate consent.

On Findora, location metadata (GPS information) is automatically removed from uploaded photos to protect your privacy.

## Edit lightly

Basic edits can improve a photo, but don't overdo it:

- Straighten and crop.
- Adjust brightness and contrast slightly.
- Avoid heavy filters that change colours — customers want to see the real result.

Never edit photos in a way that misrepresents your work. Honest photos protect your reputation.

## Choose the right photos for your profile

For your Findora profile:

- **Logo:** a square image with a plain background works best.
- **Cover photo:** a wide, landscape image representing your business at its best.
- **Gallery:** six to twelve photos covering your work, premises and team. Write a short description for each — it helps customers using screen readers and gives search engines context.

## Update regularly

Add new photos as you complete interesting jobs. A fresh gallery shows customers you're active and gives them more reasons to choose you.

With clean lenses, good light, tidy backgrounds and honest edits, your phone can produce photos that make your business look as professional as it really is.
