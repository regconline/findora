Technology should make running your business easier, not more complicated. You don't need expensive systems to work efficiently — many effective tools are free or cost less than a weekly fuel top-up. This guide covers the categories that matter most for small businesses and what to look for in each.

## 1. Business communication

**WhatsApp Business** is the starting point for many South African businesses. Useful features include:

- A business profile with your address, hours and website.
- Quick replies for common questions.
- Labels to organise customers (new enquiry, quoted, paid).
- A catalogue of products or services.

A **professional email address** using your own domain (for example info@yourbusiness.co.za) looks more credible than a free personal address and is inexpensive to set up with most hosting providers.

## 2. Quoting and invoicing

Handwritten invoices get lost and look unprofessional. Cloud accounting and invoicing tools let you:

- Create branded quotes and invoices in minutes.
- Track which invoices are unpaid.
- Send automatic payment reminders.
- Keep records ready for your accountant and for SARS.

Many offer free or low-cost plans for very small businesses. Choose one that your accountant is comfortable working with.

## 3. Bookings and scheduling

If you work by appointment — salons, consultants, tutors, health practitioners — an online booking tool can reduce back-and-forth messages. Look for:

- Automatic reminders to reduce no-shows.
- Calendar sync with your phone.
- The option to take deposits for certain services.

Even a shared digital calendar is a big step up from a paper diary.

## 4. Accepting payments

Customers increasingly expect to pay by card or instant EFT. Options include card machines that connect to your phone, payment links you can send by WhatsApp or email, and QR code payments. Compare transaction fees, settlement times and monthly costs before choosing.

## 5. File storage and backups

Store important documents — registration certificates, contracts, quotes, invoices and photos — in a cloud storage service rather than only on one phone or laptop. If a device is lost or stolen, your records are safe.

Organise files into simple folders (Clients, Suppliers, Finance, Compliance) and name files consistently so you can find them quickly.

## 6. Productivity and collaboration

If you work with a team, shared documents, spreadsheets and task lists help everyone stay on the same page. A simple shared list of jobs with statuses ("to quote", "booked", "in progress", "invoiced") can replace dozens of messages.

## 7. Online presence

Your online presence is also a technology tool:

- A complete Findora business profile that customers can find by service and location.
- A Google Business Profile for map searches.
- A simple website if your business benefits from more detailed information.

On Findora, your dashboard also shows real statistics — profile views, call and WhatsApp clicks, and enquiries — so you can see which efforts are working.

## 8. Cybersecurity basics

Small businesses are targets for scams and account takeovers. Protect yourself with a few habits:

- **Use strong, unique passwords** and a password manager.
- **Turn on two-factor authentication** for email, banking, social media and WhatsApp.
- **Be suspicious of urgent payment requests**, especially changed banking details sent by email. Phone the supplier on a known number to confirm.
- **Keep devices updated** with the latest software.
- **Back up regularly.**

## 9. Choosing tools wisely

Before signing up for anything, ask:

1. What problem does this solve, and how much time will it save?
2. What does it cost per month, including transaction fees?
3. Is it easy to cancel, and can I export my data?
4. Does it work well on a phone?
5. How does the provider protect my and my customers' data?

Start with one tool, use it properly for a month, then add the next.

## Final thought

The best technology is the technology you actually use. Choose simple tools that remove repetitive work, keep your records organised and help customers reach you — then spend the time you save on growing your business.
