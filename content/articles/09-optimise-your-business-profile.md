Your business profile often creates the first impression a customer has of your business. In a few seconds they decide whether to call you or move on to the next listing. This guide walks through each part of your Findora profile and how to make it work harder for you.

## 1. Choose the right category and subcategory

Customers often browse by category, and search results consider your category and subcategory. Choose the option that best describes your **main** service. If you offer several types of service, use the services section to cover the others — each service can be assigned its own service category.

## 2. Write a clear, specific description

Your description should answer four questions in the first few sentences:

1. **What do you do?**
2. **Who do you do it for?**
3. **Where do you operate?**
4. **Why should customers choose you?**

Compare these two descriptions:

> "We provide quality services at affordable prices. Contact us today."

> "Family-run plumbing business serving Klerksdorp, Orkney and Stilfontein since 2012. We install and repair geysers, fix leaks and unblock drains for homes and small businesses, with a 12-month workmanship guarantee."

The second one is specific, builds trust and includes words customers actually search for.

Keep paragraphs short, avoid capital letters for emphasis, and don't copy your description from another business.

## 3. Add a short tagline

The tagline appears near your name and in search results. Use it to state your speciality in one line, for example "Emergency electrical repairs in Mahikeng — 7 days a week."

## 4. List every important service

Services help customers understand exactly what you offer and help your profile appear in more searches. For each service:

- Use a clear name customers would recognise ("Wheel alignment", not "WA service").
- Add a one- or two-sentence description.
- Choose a price type: fixed, starting from, hourly, quotation required, or free.
- Add availability if relevant ("Weekdays, by appointment").

You don't have to publish prices. However, "starting from" prices help customers decide whether to contact you and reduce enquiries that don't fit your pricing.

## 5. Upload a logo and cover photo

Your logo appears in search results and on your profile, so make sure it is clear at a small size. Use a square image with a plain background where possible.

Your cover photo sits at the top of your profile. Choose a wide, high-quality image that represents your business — your shopfront, team, vehicle or a strong example of your work.

## 6. Build a gallery of real photos

Add photos that show:

- Completed work (before and after is ideal).
- Your premises or workspace.
- Equipment, products or vehicles.
- Your team at work.

Write a short description for each photo. Descriptions help customers who use screen readers and give search engines more context.

## 7. Complete your contact details and hours

Add every channel you actively monitor — phone, WhatsApp, email and website — and your social media links. Then set your operating hours. Customers are more likely to contact a business when they know it's open.

Only list numbers you answer. An unanswered phone can cost you more than a missing one.

## 8. Get your location right

Select your province and city, add your suburb, and list the other areas you serve. If you work from home, you can hide your street address while still appearing in searches for your area. Adding map coordinates enables the "Get Directions" button for customers who visit you.

## 9. Respond to enquiries quickly

When a customer submits an enquiry, you'll receive an email and see it in your dashboard. Fast, friendly responses dramatically increase your chances of winning the job. Mark enquiries as read and archive them when they're dealt with to keep your inbox manageable.

## 10. Review your statistics monthly

Your dashboard shows profile views, phone clicks, WhatsApp clicks, website clicks and enquiries. Use them to improve:

- **Few views?** Check your category, service names and location details.
- **Views but few contacts?** Improve your description, photos and pricing information.
- **Many calls but few enquiries?** That's fine — customers are reaching you directly.

## 11. Keep it current

Update your profile whenever your hours, services, prices or contact details change. An accurate profile builds trust; an outdated one frustrates customers.

## Quick checklist

- [ ] Correct category and subcategory
- [ ] Specific description and tagline
- [ ] All main services listed
- [ ] Logo, cover photo and at least six gallery photos
- [ ] Phone, WhatsApp and/or email
- [ ] Operating hours
- [ ] Location and service areas

A complete, honest and up-to-date profile is one of the most effective free marketing tools available to your business.
