Findora is free for customers and offers free business listings. To sustain the Platform, we may display advertising and offer featured or sponsored placements. This policy explains our principles.

## Our principles

1. **Clear labelling.** Advertisements are labelled "Advertisement". Paid placements of business listings are labelled "Featured" or "Sponsored".
2. **Organic results stay organic.** Normal search results are ranked by relevance and the usefulness of business information. Payment does not change a listing's organic ranking.
3. **No ads in private spaces.** We do not show advertisements on sign-in or registration pages, inside dashboards, in enquiry forms or in private communications.
4. **No disguised ads.** Ads are never designed to look like navigation, search results or business listings.
5. **Privacy first.** Personalised advertising cookies are used only with your consent. See our [Cookie Policy](/cookies).

## Third-party advertising

We may use Google AdSense to display ads. Google's ad content is subject to Google's own publisher and advertising policies. If you see an ad that seems inappropriate or misleading, please tell us through our [contact page](/contact).

## Direct advertising and sponsored placements

Businesses or organisations that advertise directly with Findora must ensure their adverts:

- Are truthful, not misleading and comply with South African law, including the Consumer Protection Act and applicable advertising codes.
- Don't promote prohibited goods or services listed in our [listing guidelines](/listing-guidelines).
- Don't contain offensive, discriminatory or sexually explicit content.
- Link to legitimate destinations that match the advert.

We may decline or remove any advertisement at our discretion.

## Featured listings

Featured listings may receive additional visibility, such as placement in a labelled "Featured listings" section. Featuring is not an endorsement or guarantee, and featured businesses must meet the same listing guidelines as all other businesses.

## Questions

For advertising enquiries, choose "Advertising" on our [contact form](/contact).
