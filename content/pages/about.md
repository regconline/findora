## Our purpose

South Africa is full of capable businesses — from family-run workshops and independent professionals to growing companies and township entrepreneurs. Too often, customers simply can't find them. Findora exists to close that gap.

We help customers **discover businesses, explore services and connect directly** with service providers across South Africa. And we help businesses of every size present themselves professionally and reach new customers — free of charge.

## What makes Findora different

- **Free business listings.** Any legitimate business can create a complete profile with services, photos, operating hours and contact details at no cost.
- **Direct connection.** Customers call, WhatsApp, email or send enquiries straight to the business. We don't take commission on the work you agree.
- **Reviewed listings.** Every listing is reviewed by our team before it is published, and anyone can report a listing that looks suspicious or inaccurate.
- **Honest information.** We don't publish fake reviews or invented statistics, and we only show verification badges after a real verification process.
- **Privacy by design.** We collect only what we need, never sell personal information, and give every user tools to export or delete their data.

## Who we serve

**Customers** looking for a plumber in Polokwane, a dentist in Durban, a caterer in Kimberley or an accountant in Cape Town can search by service, category and location and compare businesses side by side.

**Businesses** — sole proprietors, freelancers, small companies, non-profits and franchises — can showcase their services, receive enquiries and see real statistics on how customers interact with their profile.

## How we sustain the platform

Listings are free. In future, Findora may offer optional premium features and clearly labelled advertising or featured placements. Organic search results will always be ranked on relevance and useful information, and any paid placement will be clearly marked as featured or sponsored.
