## For customers

### 1. Search or browse

Use the search bar to look for a business name, a service (such as "geyser installation" or "wheel alignment") or a keyword. You can also browse by [category](/categories) and narrow results by province, town, business type, price range and weekend availability.

You don't need an account to search, view profiles or contact businesses.

### 2. Compare businesses

Each business profile shows:

- A description of the business and its category.
- Services, with prices where the business has chosen to publish them.
- Operating hours, location and the areas served.
- Photos of the business and its work.
- Contact options and social media links.

Featured or sponsored listings, when offered, are always clearly labelled. Normal search results are ranked by relevance and the quality of the business information.

### 3. Connect directly

Choose how you'd like to get in touch:

- **Call Now** to speak to the business immediately.
- **WhatsApp** to send a message.
- **Send Enquiry** to submit a written request through Findora. The business receives it by email and in its dashboard, and replies to you directly.
- **Visit Website** or **Get Directions** where available.

### 4. Keep track (optional)

Create a free customer account to save favourite businesses and see the history of the enquiries you've sent.

## For businesses

### 1. Create your free account

[Register your business](/register/business) with your name, email and a password, and verify your email address.

### 2. Build your profile

Our step-by-step setup guides you through your business information, location, contact details and operating hours, branding and photos, and services and prices. Your progress is saved after each step.

### 3. Submit for review

When your profile is complete, submit it for publication. Our team checks every listing against our [listing guidelines](/listing-guidelines) — usually within two working days — and emails you the outcome.

### 4. Receive enquiries and grow

Once approved, your profile has its own web page that customers can find on Findora and through search engines. Your dashboard shows enquiries and real statistics such as profile views and calls, WhatsApp and website clicks, so you can see what's working.

## Our commitments

- Listings are free during our launch phase, with no commission on your work.
- We never publish fake reviews, ratings or statistics.
- We protect personal information in line with POPIA.
- Anyone can [report a listing](/report) that looks suspicious or incorrect.
