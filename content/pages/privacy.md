This policy explains how we process personal information when you use findora.co.za and related services (the "Platform"). We process personal information in accordance with the Protection of Personal Information Act 4 of 2013 (POPIA).

## 1. Information we collect

**Information you give us**

- **Account information:** name, email address, phone number (optional for customers), password (stored securely in hashed form by our authentication provider) and your account type.
- **Profile information you choose to add:** profile photo, city, province and a short bio.
- **Business listing information:** business name, description, category, registration number (optional), address and service areas, contact details, operating hours, services, prices, photographs and social media links. Listings are intended to be public.
- **Enquiries:** your name, email, optional phone number, subject and message when you contact a business through the Platform.
- **Reports and contact messages:** information you include when reporting a listing or contacting us.
- **Consent records:** when you accept our terms, opt in to marketing, consent to cookies or consent to the publication of business contact details.

**Information collected automatically**

- **Essential cookies** needed to keep you signed in and secure.
- **Privacy-conscious usage statistics:** counts of profile views, searches and contact-button clicks. These are linked to a salted, daily-rotating anonymous identifier, not to your name, and we do not store your IP address with them.
- **Analytics and advertising cookies** (for example Google Analytics or Google AdSense) only if you consent through our cookie preferences.
- **Server and security logs** used to protect the Platform against abuse.

## 2. Why we use personal information

We process personal information to:

1. Create and manage your account and authenticate you.
2. Publish business listings that businesses submit for publication.
3. Deliver your enquiries to the business you contact and send you a confirmation.
4. Send service emails, such as verification, password reset, listing decisions and enquiry notifications.
5. Moderate listings, investigate reports and prevent fraud, spam and abuse.
6. Provide businesses with aggregated statistics about their profiles.
7. Improve the Platform using aggregated or anonymised information.
8. Send marketing communications, only where you have opted in.
9. Comply with legal obligations.

Our lawful grounds include your consent, the performance of our agreement with you, our legitimate interests in operating a safe and useful directory, and compliance with the law.

## 3. Sharing personal information

- **With businesses you contact:** your enquiry details are shared with the business you choose to contact, so that it can reply to you.
- **Public listings:** business information submitted for publication is visible to the public. Businesses must confirm they are authorised to publish contact details.
- **Service providers (operators)** who process information on our behalf under appropriate agreements, including hosting (Vercel), database, authentication and file storage (Supabase), transactional email (Resend) and, where enabled and consented to, analytics and advertising services (Google).
- **Legal requirements:** where required by law or to protect rights, safety or property.

We do not sell personal information.

## 4. Cross-border transfers

Some of our service providers store or process information outside South Africa. Where this happens, we take steps to ensure that the recipient is subject to laws, binding agreements or corporate rules that provide an adequate level of protection, as required by section 72 of POPIA.

## 5. Security

We use reasonable technical and organisational measures, including encrypted connections (HTTPS), access controls enforced at database level, hashed passwords, restricted administrative access, audit logs of sensitive administrative actions, rate limiting and spam protection. No system is completely secure; if a security compromise affects your personal information, we will notify you and the Information Regulator as required by POPIA.

## 6. Retention

We keep personal information only for as long as needed for the purposes above or as required by law. When you delete your account, we delete your profile and personal account information and remove listings that you were the only owner of. Enquiries you sent to businesses remain with the businesses that received them. Limited records, such as security logs and records of data requests, may be kept for a reasonable period for legal and security purposes.

## 7. Your rights

Subject to POPIA, you have the right to:

- Access the personal information we hold about you — use **Download my data** in your account settings or contact us.
- Request correction or deletion of your personal information — most information can be edited in your dashboard, and you can delete your account at any time.
- Object to processing based on legitimate interests and to direct marketing.
- Withdraw consent at any time (this doesn't affect processing that happened before withdrawal).
- Lodge a complaint with the Information Regulator (South Africa): [inforegulator.org.za](https://inforegulator.org.za).

To exercise your rights, contact our Information Officer at [privacy email address] or use our [contact form](/contact).

## 8. Children

The Platform is not intended for children under 18. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children without the consent of a competent person.

## 9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy. We will post the updated version on this page and, for material changes, notify registered users by email.
