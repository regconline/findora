## Getting started

- **Customers** can search and contact businesses without an account. Create a free account to save businesses and track your enquiries.
- **Businesses** can [create a free listing](/list-your-business). Listings are reviewed before publication.

## Account help

- **Didn't receive your verification email?** Check your spam folder, then request a new link from the verification page or log-in screen.
- **Forgot your password?** Use [Forgot password](/forgot-password) to receive a secure reset link.
- **Want to change your details?** Go to Dashboard → Account Settings.
- **Want a copy of your data?** Go to Dashboard → Account Settings → Download my data.
- **Want to delete your account?** Go to Dashboard → Account Settings → Delete account. This also removes listings you are the only owner of.

## Listing help

- **Editing your listing:** Dashboard → My Businesses → select your business. Changes to published listings appear immediately.
- **Why is my listing pending?** New listings are reviewed, usually within two working days.
- **My listing was rejected:** the email and your dashboard explain why. Update the listing and resubmit it.
- **Adding photos:** use JPG, PNG or WebP images up to 4 MB. Images are resized and optimised automatically, and location metadata is removed for your privacy.
- **Removing your listing:** you can unpublish or delete it from the listing's management page at any time.

## Enquiries

- Enquiries sent through Findora are delivered to the business by email and appear in its dashboard. The business replies to you directly.
- Findora is not a party to agreements between customers and businesses. Always confirm prices, qualifications and terms before paying.

## Safety

Read our guide on [how to spot and avoid service scams](/blog/how-to-avoid-service-scams). If a listing looks suspicious, use **Report this listing** on the profile.

## Still need help?

[Contact us](/contact) and we'll respond as soon as possible, usually within two working days.
