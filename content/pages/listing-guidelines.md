Every listing is reviewed before it is published. Listings that don't meet these guidelines may be rejected, edited, hidden, suspended or removed. We may update these guidelines from time to time.

## Who may list

- Legitimate businesses, professionals and organisations operating in South Africa.
- The person creating the listing must own the business or be authorised to represent it.
- Each physical business or distinct service business should have one listing. Duplicate listings will be merged or removed.

## Accurate information

- Use your real business name — no added keywords, slogans or locations in the name field.
- Describe your business honestly and specifically.
- Choose the category that best describes your main service.
- Only list services you actually provide.
- Prices, if shown, must be genuine and must include VAT if you are VAT-registered and charge it.
- Keep your contact details, hours and location up to date.

## Contact details and personal information

- Only publish contact details you are authorised to share publicly.
- If you work from home, you may hide your street address.
- Do not publish other people's personal information without their permission.

## Photographs

Photos must:

- Be your own, or you must have permission to use them.
- Accurately represent your business, premises, products or work.
- Not contain nudity, violence, offensive material or personal information of others without consent.
- Not be misleading stock images presented as your own work.

## Registrations and claims

- Only mention registrations, accreditations, awards or memberships you actually hold. We may ask for proof.
- Do not claim to be "verified by Findora" unless your listing shows a verification badge. Badges are added only by our team after a verification process.
- Do not publish fake reviews, testimonials or ratings.

## Prohibited listings

We do not publish listings for:

- Illegal goods or services, or businesses requiring a licence they don't hold.
- Weapons, drugs or controlled substances (other than lawfully registered pharmacies and medical practices).
- Adult sexual services.
- Gambling services that are not licensed in South Africa.
- Pyramid schemes, get-rich-quick offers or unregistered financial services.
- Hate speech, discrimination or content that incites harm.
- Listings intended to impersonate another business.

## Regulated professions

If your profession is regulated — for example health professionals, legal practitioners, financial advisors, security service providers, electricians or builders — you are responsible for complying with your regulator's rules, including any rules on advertising.

## Enquiries and customer data

Use customer enquiry details only to respond to that enquiry and provide your service, in line with the Protection of Personal Information Act (POPIA). Do not add customers to marketing lists without their consent.

## Reporting and takedowns

Anyone can [report a listing](/report). We review reports and may contact the business, request corrections, hide content or remove the listing. If you believe your listing was removed in error, please [contact us](/contact).
