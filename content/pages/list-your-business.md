## What you get with a free listing

- **Your own profile page** with a search-engine-friendly web address, such as findora.co.za/businesses/your-business-name-town.
- **Services and prices** — list everything you offer, with fixed, "starting from", hourly or quotation-only pricing.
- **Photos** — your logo, a cover photo and a gallery of your work.
- **Contact buttons** — Call Now, WhatsApp, email, website and directions.
- **Enquiry inbox** — customers can send written enquiries that arrive by email and in your dashboard.
- **Real analytics** — see profile views, phone and WhatsApp clicks, website clicks and enquiries over time.
- **Full control** — edit your information whenever you like, or unpublish and delete your listing at any time.

## How to create your listing

1. **Register** with your name, email and phone number, then verify your email.
2. **Add your business details** — name, description, category and business type.
3. **Set your location** — province, town and the areas you serve.
4. **Add contact details and hours** — and confirm you're authorised to publish them.
5. **Upload your branding** — logo, cover photo and gallery.
6. **List your services** — with descriptions and prices if you choose.
7. **Review** your profile preview.
8. **Submit** for approval. We'll email you when it's live.

Most businesses finish in 10–15 minutes. Your progress is saved after each step.

## Who can list

Any legitimate business, professional or organisation operating in South Africa — sole proprietors, freelancers, private companies, partnerships, non-profits and franchises. Your business doesn't need to be CIPC-registered to list, but all information must be accurate. Please read our [listing guidelines](/listing-guidelines) before you start.

## Tips for a profile that attracts enquiries

- Write a specific description: what you do, who you serve, where, and why customers should choose you.
- Add at least six real photos of your work.
- List your main services with clear names.
- Keep your hours and contact details up to date.
- Reply to enquiries quickly.
