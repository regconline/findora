Cookies are small text files stored in your browser. Findora uses as few cookies as possible and only uses non-essential cookies with your consent.

## Essential cookies (always on)

| Cookie | Purpose | Duration |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Authentication cookies (sb-…) | Keep you signed in securely and refresh your session | Session / up to the session lifetime set by our authentication provider |
| findora_consent | Remembers your cookie choices | 6 months |

These cookies are necessary for the Platform to work and cannot be switched off.

## First-party statistics (no cookies)

We count profile views, searches and contact-button clicks to give businesses statistics and improve Findora. These counts **do not use cookies**: they use a salted, anonymous identifier that changes every day and is not linked to your name or stored with your IP address.

## Analytics cookies (optional)

If you accept analytics cookies, we use Google Analytics 4 to understand how visitors use the Platform (for example which pages are visited). IP anonymisation is requested. Google may process this information outside South Africa.

## Advertising cookies (optional)

If advertising is enabled on the Platform and you accept advertising cookies, Google AdSense may use cookies to show personalised ads. If you don't accept, any ads shown are non-personalised. Advertisements are never shown on sign-in, registration, dashboard or enquiry screens.

## Managing your choices

You can change your choices at any time using **Cookie preferences** in the footer of every page. You can also delete or block cookies in your browser settings, although blocking essential cookies will prevent you from signing in.

For more about how we handle personal information, see our [Privacy Policy](/privacy).
